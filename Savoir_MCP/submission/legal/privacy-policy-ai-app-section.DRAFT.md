## Savoir Properties app in ChatGPT and other AI assistants

You can search Savoir's property listings through our app in third-party AI assistants such as ChatGPT (the "App").

**What the App receives.** When you ask the assistant about properties, the assistant sends the App only the search criteria it needs. Examples are the area, buy or rent, property type, number of bedrooms, price range, or the reference of a listing you want to see. The App does not receive your name, email address, phone number, payment details, location or conversation history, and it does not ask for them.

**What we record.** To operate and secure the service, our servers keep technical logs:
- **App logs:** which tool was used, whether it succeeded, and how long it took. They contain no search terms and no personal data.
- **Web server logs:** standard request information such as the requesting IP address, time and requested path. Requests normally come from the AI provider's servers rather than from your device.

**Purposes.** We use this data to answer your request, keep the service running and secure, and fix errors.

**Recipients.** We do not sell this data. Our hosting provider ([Hostinger International Ltd.]) processes it on our behalf. Your conversation with the assistant, including anything you type, is handled by the AI provider under its own privacy policy (for ChatGPT, see OpenAI's privacy policy). Savoir does not receive your conversation.

**Retention.** App logs and web server logs are kept for **[30] days** and then deleted.

**Images and links.** Listing photos in the App are loaded by the assistant directly from our image providers ([Property Finder's image CDN, Cloudinary, Amazon S3]). If you open a WhatsApp, website or email link from the App, that service's own privacy policy applies.

**Contacting us about a property.** The App does not send your details to Savoir. If you contact us yourself, for example by WhatsApp, phone or email, we handle that information as described in the rest of this Privacy Policy.

**Your rights.** You can ask us about any data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, using the contact details in this policy.
