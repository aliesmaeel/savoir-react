## Savoir Properties app in ChatGPT and other AI assistants

You can search Savoir's property listings through our app in third-party AI assistants such as ChatGPT (the "App").

**What the App receives.** When you ask the assistant about properties, the assistant sends the App only the search criteria it needs. Examples are the area, buy or rent, property type, number of bedrooms, price range, or the reference of a listing you want to see. The App does not receive your name, email address, phone number, payment details, location or conversation history, and it does not ask for them.

**What we record.** To operate and secure the service, our servers keep technical logs:
- **App logs:** which tool was used, whether it succeeded, and how long it took. They contain no search terms and no personal data.
- **Web server logs:** standard request information such as the requesting IP address, time and requested path. Searches come from the AI provider's servers. However, when **you** press a Website or WhatsApp button in the App, or open a shared shortlist page, your device connects to our server, so the log records your IP address.

**Shortlists.** If you save listings, we store the listing references (no personal details) for 30 days after your last change. You can delete the shortlist at any time. If you create a share link, anyone with the link can see those listings until it expires or you stop sharing.

**Usage statistics.** We count how the App is used, for example how many searches mention an area, which budget ranges find no match, or how many times a WhatsApp button is pressed. These are daily totals. They contain no personal details, no identifiers and nothing you typed. We keep them for up to 13 months to improve our service and our listings.

**Purposes.** We use this data to answer your request, keep the service running and secure, fix errors, and understand which properties and areas customers are interested in.

**Recipients.** We do not sell this data. Our hosting provider ([Hostinger International Ltd.]) processes it on our behalf. Your conversation with the assistant, including anything you type, is handled by the AI provider under its own privacy policy (for ChatGPT, see OpenAI's privacy policy). Savoir does not receive your conversation.

**Retention.** App logs and web server logs are kept for **[30] days** and then deleted. Shortlists are kept for 30 days after their last change. Usage statistics, which contain no personal data, are kept for up to 13 months.

**Images and links.** Listing photos in the App are loaded by the assistant directly from our image providers ([Property Finder's image CDN, Cloudinary, Amazon S3]). If you open a WhatsApp, website or email link from the App, that service's own privacy policy applies.

**Contacting us about a property.** The App can prepare a message about the listings you choose, with a reference code, for you to send yourself by WhatsApp or email. The App does not send anything to Savoir and does not receive your contact details. If you contact us yourself, for example by WhatsApp, phone or email, we handle that information as described in the rest of this Privacy Policy.

**Your rights.** You can ask us about any data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, using the contact details in this policy.
